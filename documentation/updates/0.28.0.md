# 0.28.0 - Dungeon Delving
Where mana gathers thickest, space itself begins to fold. Dungeons are rare pockets of space that seal themselves behind a barrier no weapon or spell can breach from the outside.
- Over 70 named dungeons can appear across the world once a region reaches maximum Mana Density, each with its own theme, layer count and art
- Use the new decision to plan a delve: pick one of three Dungeon Party Leaders and split your party points between Cohesion, Condition and Renown
- Each layer brings events, notifications and a delve log. If the party's Condition drops to zero, the delve fails
- Clearing a dungeon opens it for exploitation which can provide gold through taxed access for adventurers or regional resource taxes (iron, lead, coal, sulfur, copper)
